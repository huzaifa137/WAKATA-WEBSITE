Common questions

Quick answers about payment, delivery and school orders.

How do I pay?

Pay with MTN Mobile Money, Airtel Money, Visa or Mastercard. You can also pay in cash when you collect from our shop.

How fast do PDF downloads arrive?

PDFs are emailed as soon as your payment is confirmed.

Do you deliver outside Kampala?

Yes. Choose upcountry delivery at checkout and we will send your order by courier.

Can my school get a bulk price?

Yes. Send us a message with the titles and quantities and we will send a quotation.

Can I collect my order from the shop?

Yes. Pickup is free. Choose pickup at checkout and collect from us during opening hours.

When are you open?

Monday to Saturday, 8am to 6pm. Messages sent outside these hours are answered the next working day.

Still have a question?

Call, write or send us a message. We reply within one working day.
