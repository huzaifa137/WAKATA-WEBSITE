About WAKATA

The Wakiso Kampala Teachers' Association brings teachers together to make good assessment affordable for every learner.

Written by teachers, for teachers

WAKATA is an association of teachers from Wakiso and Kampala. Every paper, workbook and revision guide we publish is written and checked by people who teach the syllabus every day.

We believe a school should not need a large budget to give its candidates a proper mock examination. That is why our papers are priced for schools of every size, and why we sell them as printed copies, PDFs or whole sets.
