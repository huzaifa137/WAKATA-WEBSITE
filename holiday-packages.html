Holiday packages

Workbooks, revision tests and mock papers in one bundle, so learning carries on while school is out.

Questions about packages

Quick answers about ordering, swaps and what is inside.

When should I order a holiday package?

Order at least one week before school closes so your package is ready for collection or delivery on the first day of the holiday.

Can I swap a book in the package?

Yes. Tell us at checkout or call us, and we will swap a title for another of the same level.

Do packages include answers?

Every test and mock paper in a package comes with a marking guide for the parent or teacher.

Can I collect my package from the shop?

Yes. Pickup is free. Choose pickup at checkout and collect from us during opening hours.

Need help choosing a package?

Tell us the level and subjects and we will suggest the right one.
